## Definition
[[Copula|Copulas]] with closed-form expressions are called **explicit copulas**. A major family of explicit copulas is the **Archimedean** family. Let $\psi_\theta:[0,1]\to[0,\infty]$ be continuous, convex, and strictly decreasing, with $\psi_\theta(1)=0$ and $\psi_\theta^{-1}(t)=0$ for $t\geq\psi_\theta(0)$ ($\psi_\theta$ is called the **generator function**). The Archimedean copula generated by $\psi_\theta$ is
$$
C(u,v) = \psi_\theta^{-1}\left(\psi_\theta(u)+\psi_\theta(v)\right)
$$
where $\theta$ is a dependence parameter.

## Properties
### Clayton copula
$$
C_\theta(u,v) = \max\left(u^{-\theta}+v^{-\theta}-1,\;0\right)^{-1/\theta}, \qquad \theta\in[-1,\infty)\setminus\{0\}
$$
generator $\psi_\theta(u) = (u^{-\theta}-1)/\theta$.

### Frank copula
$$
C_\theta(u,v) = \frac{1}{\ln\theta}\ln\left[1+\frac{(\theta^u-1)(\theta^v-1)}{\theta-1}\right], \qquad \theta>0
$$
generator $\psi_\theta(u) = -\ln\left(\dfrac{\theta^u-1}{\theta-1}\right)$.

### Gumbel copula
$$
C_\theta(u,v) = \exp\left\{-\left[(-\ln u)^\theta+(-\ln v)^\theta\right]^{1/\theta}\right\}, \qquad \theta\in[1,\infty)
$$
generator $\psi_\theta(u) = (-\ln u)^\theta$.

### Joe copula
$$
C_\theta(u,v) = 1-\left[(1-u)^\theta+(1-v)^\theta-(1-u)^\theta(1-v)^\theta\right]^{1/\theta}, \qquad \theta\in[1,\infty)
$$
generator $\psi_\theta(u) = -\ln\left(1-(1-u)^\theta\right)$.

### The independence copula is Archimedean
$C_\theta(u,v)=uv$ (the [[Copula|independence copula]] $\Pi$) is Archimedean with generator $\psi_\theta(u)=-\ln u$: $\psi_\theta^{-1}(\psi_\theta(u)+\psi_\theta(v)) = \exp(-(-\ln u - \ln v)) = uv$.

### Choosing among copulas
Two practical approaches: **visual inspection** — transform the data's margins to uniform and plot on $[0,1]^2$, then compare the scatter pattern to that of a candidate copula; or **compare likelihoods** — fit several candidate copulas by maximum likelihood and select by highest likelihood (or AIC).
