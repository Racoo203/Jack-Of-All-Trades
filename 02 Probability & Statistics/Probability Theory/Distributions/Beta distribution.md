## [[Probability Density Function (PDF)]]
$$
f(x | \alpha, \beta) = \frac{1}{B(\alpha, \beta)}x^{\alpha - 1}(1-x)^{\beta - 1}
$$
Where  $x \in [0,1]$, and $\alpha, \beta > 0$.
