## Definition
A **swap** is an agreement to exchange, at specified future dates, financial assets or cash flows. Two main types:
- **Interest rate swaps**: swapping only the future cash flows relating to interest payments on loans (no exchange of principal).
- **Currency swaps**: at inception, swapping the actual principals in different currencies; swapping the interest-related cash flows during the life of the swap; and reversing the swapped principals at the end.
