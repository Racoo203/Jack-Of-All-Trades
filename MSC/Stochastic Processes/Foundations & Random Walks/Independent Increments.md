Independent increments are a property of stochastic processes. These processes often have the property by definition.

## Definition
Let $\{X_t, t \in T\}$ be a [[Stochastic Process|stochastic process]]. Such stochastic process has independent increments if and only if for any $t_1 < \dots < t_n$ s.t. $t_0, t_n \in T$, then
$$
(X_{t_1}), (X_{t_2} - X_{t_1}), \cdots, (X_{t_n} - X_{t_{n-1}})
$$
Are [[Stochastic Independence|independent]] [[Random Variable|random variables]].
