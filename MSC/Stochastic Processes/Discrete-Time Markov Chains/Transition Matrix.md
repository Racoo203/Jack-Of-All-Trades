## Definition
The matrix $P_n$ which entries are the transition probabilities $p_{ij, n}$,
$$
P_n = \begin{bmatrix}
p_{11, n} & p_{12, n} & \cdots & p_{1j, n} & \cdots \\
p_{21, n} & p_{22, n} & \cdots & p_{2j, n} & \cdots \\
\vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \cdots & \cdots \\
p_{j1, n} & p_{j2, n} & \cdots & p_{jj, n} & \cdots \\
\vdots & \vdots & \cdots & \cdots & \ddots \\
\end{bmatrix}
$$
is called the transition matrix at step $n$.
