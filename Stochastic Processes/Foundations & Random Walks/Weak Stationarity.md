## Definition
A [[Stochastic Process|stochastic process]] $\{X_t : t \in T\}$ is weakly stationary if:
- $\mu_X(t) = \mu_X$,
- $C_X(t_1, t_2) = C(t_2 - t_1)$, with $t_1, t_2 \in T$, and
- $E[|X_t|^2] < \infty$.

That is: 1. The [[Mean Function|mean function]] is constant; 2. The cross moment function (covariance) of the process is a function of the time differences, and; 3. The second moments are finite at any time.
