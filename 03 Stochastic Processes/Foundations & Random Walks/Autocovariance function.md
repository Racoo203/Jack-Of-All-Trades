## Definition
For a given [[Stochastic Process|stochastic process]] $\{X_t : t \in T \}$, the autocovariance function $C_X(t_1, t_2)$ is the [[Expected Value|expected value]] of the product of deviations of [[Random Variable|random variables]] from the mean at times $t_1, t_2$. That is,
$$
C_X(t_1, t_2) = E[(X_{t_1} - \mu_X(t_1))(X_{t_2} - \mu_X(t_2))]
$$
