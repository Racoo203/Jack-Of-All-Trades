## Definition
For a given [[Stochastic Process|stochastic process]] $\{X_t : t \in T \}$, the mean function $\mu_X(t)$ is the [[Expected Value|expected value]] of the [[Random Variable|random variable]] at time $t$. That is,
$$
\mu_X(t) = E[X_t]
$$
